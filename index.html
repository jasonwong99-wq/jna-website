Mum Hero

Your Family's Second BrainiOS first · Android to followMalaysia

Screenshot the class group. Mum Hero remembers the rest. Share a WhatsApp notice, photo or PDF from school, tuition or the music class, and Mum Hero pulls out the fees, exam dates, things to bring and activities, split per child, with the reminders already set.

  • Reads screenshots, photos and PDFs in any mix of English, Chinese and Malay. One notice, split per child.
  • Reminders that fit the job: fees 7, 3 and 1 day before; things to bring, the night before.
  • Up to 6 family members: husband, grandma, helper, each with their own role.
  • Ask Mum Hero "what does Alex need the day after tomorrow?" and get an answer from your own family's items only.

Built by a Malaysian dad for his wife, for Malaysian parents. One family plan, shared by up to 6 people.

Eldivo

Better Care. Together.Early testing on AndroidWorldwide

One parent. Several people looking after her. One record they all trust. Eldivo is the shared care record for a family looking after an elderly parent: medicines, blood pressure, clinic visits, meals, reports and who did what, with every entry showing who recorded it and when.

  • One record the whole family sees. You decide what each family member and carer can see and do: a helper can log a dose but cannot change the prescription.
  • Show Eldivo a prescription, a blood-pressure monitor screen or a blood test report. It reads what it can, then asks you to confirm before anything is saved.
  • Photograph a meal, correct what it sees, and Eldivo checks the plate against her readings and the doctor's diet advice.
  • Files name themselves ("Blood test, 12 Sep 2026"). Blood pressure and sugar logs export to a PDF with a chart you can WhatsApp to the doctor.

Not a medical device and not an emergency tool. Eldivo does not diagnose or interpret results; what the doctor says always comes first.

CukaiSifu

Every client's tax year. One calm app.Closed beta · Android firstMalaysia

The pocket tax sifu for Malaysian freelance accountants. Forty clients, one calendar, nothing to carry in your head. Add a client once and CukaiSifu builds their whole statutory year: LHDN, SSM, SST, payroll and e-Invoice, then remembers, captures, projects and reports so you don't have to.

  • Statutory engine: enter a year end and entity type, and every recurring and annual deadline is generated
  • Capture by photo, PDF, typing or voice, even with English, Malay and Chinese in one sentence
  • Gmail reading that proposes reminders and drafts replies. Nothing is ever sent without you.
  • A morning brief, and spoken answers when you ask what is due this week
